# Infrastructure

I used to self host as much as I possibly could at one point. Unfortunately, I don’t have the time or energy to maintain a self-hosted setup anymore. Not to mention the odd power outages that happen more than I would like!

I still self host some things, but most of my projects are hosted on my NearlyFreeSpeech.NET account, TierHive VPS, or RamHost.

**Last updated**: August 2026
